Welcome to Kasswuit's Kitsu Cards!

(Note: This page is still under construction)
Here, you can learn Japanese using the Genki III curriculum. The vocabulary is sorted by chapters. In each chapter, you can select the specific categories or words you wish to learn. Then, those words will be shuffled. You will see the word presented in English, and the challenge is for you to write it in Japanese on a seperate sheet of paper. At the end, you will self grade yourself on the words you got right, and repeat the process again. You will need to get each word correct 3 times in a row before you complete that word.
